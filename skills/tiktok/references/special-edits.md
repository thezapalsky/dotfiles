# Bulk purchases, supporting photos and value summaries

Read for bulk-card purchases, questionable packaging or requested result summaries. These are optional modes, not defaults for every booster opening.

## Bulk-card pacing and overlays

- Complete a scan-first report before editing when requested. Bulk quantities can be high: inspect all reveals rather than identifying only the valuable cards. Mark obscured cards and unresolved quantities; do not silently skip them.
- Use a quicker pace than a showcase booster opening, while retaining the initial presentation of the purchase. If two stacks are shown, let both stacks enter the frame before cutting away.
- A useful opening premise is `bought this card bulk for [local cost] (≈ €X) — was it worth it?`. Substitute the actual purchase, cost and dated exchange rate; do not reuse a guessed conversion.
- If requested, flash the current card's individual EUR value and keep a separate cumulative total at the bottom. A cumulative total does not replace the individual flashes. Make the individual value prominent; keep both away from card text and TikTok UI.
- Increment the total once per physical copy at its reveal, not on every reappearance. Handle repeated copies correctly. Preserve full precision for calculations and format consistently; do not silently price unknowns at zero. Label an incomplete total as known-value subtotal or approximate total as appropriate.
- Regenerate flashes, totals and audio cues from the final reveal timeline after changing cuts or speed.

## Less repetitive sound for bulk

An example sparse bulk profile uses a full, original cash effect only for cards **strictly above €0.20** when that threshold is selected. Cards at €0.20 or below were silent apart from original handling audio. This replaces the ordinary three-tier booster cues for a project using that profile; do not play both systems.

Keep the original effect at normal speed with its natural tail. Do not truncate, accelerate or repeatedly restart it to fit every card. Use a no-overlap rule when reveals are close: finish the active effect and suppress colliding cues instead of delaying sounds onto unrelated cards. If cues still dominate the edit, propose a higher threshold or fewer highlight cues. Do not apply a median-based rule when the user selected a fixed threshold.

Use the original user-supplied effect from the project’s configured SFX directory; a trimmed derivative is not a substitute for a specifically requested original. Record asset path, threshold, tail policy and overlap handling in the project settings. Current user instructions override this example.

## Context photos and uncertain claims

Insert user-provided packaging or unsealed-pack photos when they clarify the story. Choose readable crops, provide a brief view, and avoid showing addresses, labels or other personal details unnecessarily. Preserve the originals and record insert timing in the edit manifest.

Distinguish observation from inference: `packs arrived unsealed` or `no rares/mythics visible in the scanned contents` may be supported; `the seller removed them` needs evidence or wording that makes it a suspicion. Do not invent unseen cards or call an unseen rarity definitively absent from an entire shipment. Missing normal pack contents are a reason to flag uncertainty, not fill in assumed pulls.

## Result cards and teasers

When approved, end with a short readable summary: total pull value, purchase cost and an optional reaction. A completed real action can precede a summary; do not cut off the restack or add an unwanted `boosters next` teaser.

- Show purchase cost in its original currency and approximate EUR using a dated exchange rate and documented rounding. Keep purchase cost, current sealed-product value and pull value separate.
- If adding `sealed: €X`, verify the exact product (one Collector Booster versus Play Booster or bundle), market, metric and date. Avoid presenting an asking price as realized sale proceeds.
- If comparing cost with pulls, label the result as an indicative difference; price estimates do not include selling fees/shipping unless explicitly calculated. Do not double-count land packets, promo or accessories.
- A longer, louder cheer can fit a sparse ending or major hit. Audition against original sound and prevent clipping; a fixed low gain is not a substitute for listening.
- Brief approved jokes, reactions and future-episode teasers are exceptions to value-only text, not permission to add general commentary. Verify claims such as `several cards may fit my Commander deck` against the report and describe them as candidates rather than guaranteed upgrades.
- Keep results out of the caption unless the user asks for spoilers.
